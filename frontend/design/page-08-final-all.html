What changes between phone and desktop

Four things, and nothing else: the tab bar becomes a rail; lists that were sheets become panels; anything a phone reaches with a second tap sits side by side; and the car becomes a real 3D model you can orbit.

Type sizes, colours, radii and spacing are identical. A desktop layout that also redesigns the components is two design systems wearing one name.

About the car

This is genuine three.js — low-poly geometry with edge wireframes, a warm rim, a scan sweep and the faults marked on the body. It is here to show the treatment, not the final asset.

In production it becomes a compressed glTF model. Five generic body types — sedan, hatch, SUV, pickup, minibus — cover almost every car in Uganda without licensing a model per vehicle or shipping 20MB down a mobile connection.

Still open

The display face. Orbitron is standing in for XSPACE everywhere. Swapping it touches one CSS variable.

Guest access, booking, account deletion policy, and which map SDK. Each is noted on its own page and each is cheaper to answer now than after the components exist.
