changed from V1

The question comes first

V1 asked you to sign in, then asked whether you own a car. Your plan (§3.1) has it the other way round and marks the question "No Login Required" — so that is what this does.

It is the better order for a reason worth keeping: the cheapest possible question comes before the commitment, and the account screen then arrives already personalised. Pick a different journey on step 1 and watch step 2's greeting change.

The choice explains itself

V1 offered two buttons marked YES and NO. Neither told you what the answer would change, and this answer sets the entire shape of the app.

Each option now names what you get. The reassurance underneath — you can change it later, or use both — is there because §3.2 says that is true, and someone hesitating deserves to know it.

OBD is step three, and only sometimes

§8.1 puts pairing in onboarding and marks it optional. It only appears for car owners — showing it to someone who hasn't bought a car yet would be nonsense — so this flow is three steps or two, and the rail at the top adapts.

The line about working without one is from §8.3, and it is deliberate: people skip things they think will break the app. Telling them what actually happens makes skipping an informed choice.

Password copy

The hint reads "Eight characters or more. Nothing else required." Composition rules — a symbol, a capital, a digit — measurably push people toward weaker, more predictable passwords, and length is what actually matters.

Errors on this screen should name the fix, never the failure: Use at least 8 characters — add four more.

exception

The Google logo is in colour

It is the only full-colour thing in the entire app, and it has to be: Google's brand terms require the mark in its own colours on a neutral button. Recolouring it to monochrome is not permitted.

Worth knowing so nobody later "fixes" it for consistency.

Open

No guest route. The plan ends onboarding at a completed account. If you want people trying the assistant before signing up, that decision belongs here, and it is cheap now.
