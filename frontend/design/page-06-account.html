PHRONESIS 2.0 · KAMPALA

Named after what it does for you

Not "Profile", "Settings", "Preferences". The sections are You · Journey · Your car · Voice · Alerts · Your data — each named for the thing it changes, so nobody has to guess which drawer the OBD pairing is in.

The name field says what it is for: it is what the assistant calls you when it speaks. That is not obvious, and it changes how people fill it in.

deliberate

Critical alerts cannot be switched off

Every other notification is yours to silence. Critical faults are not. If the engine is about to fail, a muted toggle is not a preference we should have honoured.

The switch is shown, disabled, with the reason written next to it — rather than hidden — so the choice is visible and explained instead of quietly missing.

One conversation, not a history list

§7.2 gives each user a single continuous thread. So this says Conversation, singular, and offers to read or export it. A "Chat history" list would promise segmentation the product does not have.

What Phronesis remembers sits beside it: an assistant that claims memory owes you a way to see and correct what it thinks it knows.

Journey lives here

This answers the open question from Home. §3.2 gives users a primary journey plus access to both — so the primary is a segmented control, and "show me both" is a separate switch underneath rather than a third option muddling the first choice.

needs your call

Delete, and the data question behind it

Delete everything is in the list rather than beside Sign out, because they are different in kind: one ends a session, the other ends the relationship. It needs a confirmation naming exactly what goes — conversation, car history, diagnoses, reviews left for mechanics.

The reviews are the hard part. If someone deletes their account, do the ratings they gave mechanics disappear too? That changes a shop's reputation retroactively, and it is a policy decision, not a UI one.
